About

I want to understand why some places prosper while others are left behind, and what business and policy can do about it.

I am from Raipur, Chhattisgarh, and much of my work so far has been rooted there. I have researched artisans’ livelihoods and the conflict in Bastar, published a paper on sustainable business models, and founded the Indravati Foundation to support government schools and children in Bastar, Kondagaon and Raipur.

I have also worked inside businesses: on construction sites with Mayank Associates, and on project planning and business development at Avinash Group. I am now developing GreenRx, a concept for an ethical pharmaceutical company.

In the long term, I intend to build a career in business, with philanthropy as a lasting part of it.
