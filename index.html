About

I want to understand why some places prosper while others are left behind, and what business and policy can do about it.

I am from Raipur, Chhattisgarh, and most of what I have done so far started there. I have researched the livelihoods of local artisans and the conflict in Bastar, published a paper on sustainable business models, and founded the Indravati Foundation, which supports government schools and children in Bastar, Kondagaon and Raipur.

I have also spent time inside working businesses: on construction sites with Mayank Associates, and in project planning and business development at Avinash Group. Right now I am developing GreenRx, an idea for a company that makes affordable, fully vegan medicines.

In the long run, I want to build a career in business and keep philanthropy as a lasting part of it.

Kosal Kala

Field pilot

A field project to help artisans in Chhattisgarh sell their work at a fair price by giving them direct access to buyers. I ran it as a pilot and closed it after that phase. It taught me about running a project on the ground, pricing handmade work and getting people to adopt something new. My paper The Artisans’ Renaissance looks at the same problem in depth.

SSX Bowers

Prototype

A prototype of a stock market simulation game built around sustainable investing, where players learn about environmental, social and governance (ESG) factors while trading with virtual money. I stopped after the first round of testing, but building it taught me about product design, keeping users engaged and judging whether an idea can work.
